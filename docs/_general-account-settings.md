# General Account Settings

In this section, we'll go through the account settings shown in your PipeOps dashboard. These settings help you access your profile, manage account security, review payment cards, connect Git providers, manage OAuth methods, and close your account when needed.

To access your profile settings, click the account button at the top-right corner of your dashboard, then select **Profile**.

<!-- PLACEHOLDER: Screenshot 1 — Account menu opened from the top-right dashboard button -->

## Profile

The **Profile** section provides a centralized interface for managing your account details, security settings, payment cards, repository provider connections, OAuth sign-in methods, and account closure options.

### Account

The **Account** area displays your basic account information. You can review your avatar, workspace name, email address, full name, and password status.

You can also use the available **Edit** actions to update editable details such as your full name or password.

<!-- PLACEHOLDER: Screenshot 2 — Profile page account, security, and debit/credit card sections -->

### Security

The **Security** area lets you manage additional sign-in protection:

- **Multi-Factor Authentication:** Shows whether MFA is enabled or disabled. Click **Enable** to set it up.
- **Passkeys:** Lets you manage passkeys for passwordless sign-in.

### Debit/Credit Card

The **Debit/Credit Card** area lists payment cards connected to your account. Each card row shows the card provider, masked card number, payment processor, and status.

To remove a card, click **Remove** beside the card you want to delete.

### Connected Provider

The **Connected Provider** area shows the Git repository providers connected to your PipeOps account for project deployments.

PipeOps supports:

- GitHub
- GitLab
- Bitbucket

If a provider is connected, you can click **Disconnect** to remove the connection. If a provider is not connected, click **Connect** to link it to your account.

<!-- PLACEHOLDER: Screenshot 3 — Profile page connected providers, OAuth methods, and danger zone sections -->

### OAuth Methods

The **OAuth Methods** area shows third-party sign-in methods linked to your account, such as GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, and Google.

### Danger Zone

The **Danger Zone** contains the option to close your account. Closing your account removes your data and projects, so only use this option when you are sure you no longer need the account.

---

The General Account Settings section in PipeOps gives you control over the profile and account-level options shown on this page, from security settings to provider connections and account closure.
