# Pricing

PipeOps pricing is designed to meet the needs of businesses of all sizes.
We offer a transparent usage-based model so you can scale without
unexpected costs. Whether you're an individual developer, a growing
startup, or a large organization, you only pay for the resources you use.

## Usage-Based Pricing

PipeOps operates on a usage-based pricing model — you are billed only
for your actual cloud resource consumption at the end of each billing
cycle. This eliminates fixed resource classes and gives you the
flexibility to scale as needed, while your subscription plan determines
the platform features and covered Nova usage available to you.

## Payment Methods

PipeOps supports a variety of payment methods to accommodate users
across different regions:

1. **Paystack** — Best for Local Payments
   - **Card Payments:** Pay securely using debit or credit cards.
   - **Bank Transfers:** Make payments directly from your bank account.
   - **USSD:** Use USSD codes for quick and convenient payments.
   - **PayAttitude:** Pay directly from your phone number.

2. **Stripe** — Best for International Payments
   - **Card Payments:** Accepts major credit and debit cards, including
     Visa, Mastercard, and American Express.
   - **Cash App Pay:** A convenient option for Cash App users.
   - **US Bank Accounts:** Direct payments from US-based bank accounts.

You can select your preferred payment method during checkout.

## FAQs

### Is there a free trial?

Yes. New users receive a 14-day free trial upon signing up. During this
period, you can explore PipeOps and its features. After the trial, you
will be billed based on the subscription tier you selected.

### What's the difference between subscription and resource usage?

Your subscription gives you access to the platform and its features,
and includes a fixed amount of covered Nova usage per month. If your
resource consumption stays within your plan's coverage, you won't pay
anything extra. If you exceed it, you'll only be billed for the excess
at the end of your billing cycle.

### Which plan is best for me?

- **Growth:** Ideal for individual developers who need essential
  features and a low-cost entry point.
- **Platform:** Suited for small teams that need more environments,
  higher deployment limits, and collaborative access.
- **Scale:** Best for larger teams requiring unlimited deployments,
  advanced features, and multi-cloud provider support.
- **Custom:** Designed for enterprises with unique requirements,
  offering fully tailored features, volume discounts, and
  dedicated support.

### Can I upgrade or downgrade at any time?

Yes. You can switch between plans at any time. Changes take effect
at the start of the next billing cycle.
