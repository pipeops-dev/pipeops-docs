# Managing Your Account

PipeOps gives you a central place to manage everything related to your account, from personal details and payment methods to notifications and security documents. You can access all of these from the account menu in the top-right corner of your dashboard.

## Profile

The Profile page lets you manage your personal information, security settings, and connected services. From here you can:

- Update your workspace name, full name, and email address
- Change your password or enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Manage Passkeys for passwordless sign-in
- Add or remove debit/credit cards linked to your account
- Connect or disconnect repository providers, including GitHub, GitLab, and Bitbucket, used for project deployments
- Manage OAuth methods for third-party sign-in

:::warning

The Danger Zone at the bottom of the Profile page contains the option to permanently close your account. This action removes all your data and projects and cannot be undone.

:::

## Teams

The Teams page lets you invite members to your PipeOps workspace, assign roles, and manage collaborative access across projects.

## Usage

The Usage page gives you a breakdown of your active subscription plan, resource consumption, and available credits across your environments.
[Learn more about Usage →](/docs/usage.md)

## Billing

The Billing page keeps your financial details transparent and manageable. From here you can:

- View your active plan and billing cycle
- Upgrade your plan or cancel your subscription
- Update your billing contact email
- Add or change your default payment method
- Review your billing history, including invoice numbers, amounts, dates, and payment status

## Settings

The Settings page is divided into two tabs: **Notifications** and **Trust Center**.

### Notifications

The Notifications section has two sub-sections:

- Alert Notifications — Configure which platform events trigger alerts. You can toggle notifications for events including: Project Created, Deployment Success, Deployment Failure, Build Success, Build Failure, Project Restarted, Rollback Triggered, Project Deleted, Addon Created, Addon Deleted, and Server Created.
- Notification Channels — Choose where your alerts are delivered. Supported channels include Email, Slack, Discord, and custom webhooks.

### Trust Center

The Trust Center provides access to PipeOps' security and compliance documents, including:

- SOC 2 Type II Report
- Penetration Test Report
- Security Whitepaper
- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Service
- Data Processing Agreement (DPA)
